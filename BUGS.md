- **Crash recovery:** Exact source `historical-validation` atomically checkpoints every completed chunk using the audio/model/runtime/chunk identity and retains the completed checkpoint until the final cache commits. A hardened real-engine proof killed the process after chunk 1/5; the next run explicitly reused chunk 1, completed 19 turns, atomically wrote the final cache, and only then removed the checkpoint.
